## Ubud Drum Page
Located at `/src/app/ubuddrum/page.tsx`

### Sections:
1. **Hero Image** - Full-width image at `/images/ubuddrum2.png`
2. **Workshop Details** - 2-column layout similar to KosmosAlbum with:
   - Image on left (`/images/drum_august.jpg`)
   - Title, description, and ticket link on right

### To update workshop events:
- Change the image in section 2
- Update the date in the description
- Update the Megatix ticket link
